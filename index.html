The Bettering Run

A 4.5KM run + post-run carnival from PAssion Wave to Vidacity — an evening of fun, health and sustainability.

What is The Bettering Run?

The Bettering Run 2026 (TBR26) is a 4.5KM run + post-run carnival returning for its 2nd edition, supported by The Bettering Branch and The Kampung Spirit Initiative.

Occurring on 13th June 2026, starting from PAssion Wave to Vidacity — a sustainability-focused innovation hub and living lab — we promise an evening of fun, health and sustainability!

Why Health & Sustainability?

Both causes are undermined by the same root behaviours of overconsumption and short-termism, and both require the same mindset shift toward long-term thinking over immediate convenience.

We organised The Bettering Run with the hopes that it would be a tangible, community-facing way to engage youths (our peers and friends), making health and sustainability feel fun rather than abstract and boring.

It also served as a way for us to unite and uplift multiple youth groups, community partners and small businesses that support Health & Sustainability all around a shared event

How is The Bettering Run Unique?

The Bettering Run was started by The Bettering Branch — a group of students not more than the age of 20 — because we wanted to give our peers a fun entry point into choosing healthier & more sustainable lives.

Vendors are here at no added cost — We have over 30 booths at TBR26! Events typically charge vendors a fee to be there, but our booth vendors are all here at no added cost. Running a small business isn't easy and so we don't charge a single cent, especially to all our vendors who are promoting more sustainable and healthy lifestyles. That said, vendors who are able to, have shared a little joy in return - in the form of exclusive vouchers & discounts for our registered runners!

~90% zero waste — We opt out of traditional decor and banners, choose not to provide single-use signage, and even picked up damaged factory-reject whiteboards for our signages — reusing them again and again at our other events!
